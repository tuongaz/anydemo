  - To trigger the peer-cap path without tripping replay protection, call `session-join` a second time on the same token to mint a fresh JWT (different nonce, different peerId). Reusing the original peerJwt from `setupSession()` would always 401 replay-detected.

  - The fake `doc.send` mock from ws-default.test.ts can be reused almost verbatim BUT add PutCommand + QueryCommand + DeleteCommand branches because session-create + session-join + endSession use those.
