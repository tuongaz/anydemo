  - `RpcResultPayloadSchema` in `share-rpc-schema.ts` is **strict** and only allows `{ ok, result?, attributedTo? }` | `{ ok:false, reason }`. The PRD wording "reply with rpc-result { ok:true, id:reqId, payload:{ via, key?, putUrl? } }" must be interpreted as "wrap via/key/putUrl in `result`": `{ ok:true, result:{ via, key?, putUrl? } }`. Peer parsers (US-063) read `payload.result.via`, not `payload.via`.

The relay-amended `payload.upload` (intent) and `payload.getUrl` (done) are STRIPPED by `.safeParse`.
